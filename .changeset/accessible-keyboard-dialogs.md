Add keyboard block insertion, accessible layer-tree navigation, and modal dialog focus management. Block items now insert at the document root with Enter or Space; layer rows support roving focus, selection, renaming, and context-menu shortcuts; export and media dialogs trap focus, close with Escape, expose modal labels, and restore focus to their trigger.
